About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the College of AI, Tsinghua University, and a research intern at NVIDIA in Singapore. Before that I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Tsinghua University.

My work centres on agent flows: the loops, reviewers and runtimes that organise coding agents for long-horizon tasks, from software engineering and machine-learning engineering to olympiad mathematics and formal proofs. I also work on LLM reasoning and efficient inference, and have long been building open-source developer tools.
